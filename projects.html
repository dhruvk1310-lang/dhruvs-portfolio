Energy & logistics

Northeast Heating & Cooling Demand Map

End-to-end pipeline estimating monthly heating oil, propane and AC demand for all 218 counties across nine Northeast states, from public Census, EIA and NOAA data.

What I did

  • Built a Python pipeline that pulls eight public Census, NOAA and EIA datasets into clean tables, including county-level monthly weather back to 1991.
  • Modeled monthly demand from homes, gallons per heating degree day and prices: an estimated $16.8B market in 2025-26 that peaks every January.
  • Built a SQLite star schema and nine window-function SQL analyses, including a dealer fit score from Connecticut license records: Western CT has 2,206 homes per dealer, the most under-served region.
  • Planned a real dealer's winter deliveries with OR-Tools routing on real road distances: 34% fewer truck miles than hand-sequenced routes, with 2 trucks and zero run-outs.
  • Published four interactive Tableau Public dashboards, all generated from code.
  • Python
  • SQL
  • SQLite
  • Tableau
  • OR-Tools
  • geopandas

Legal Motion Outcome Prediction - NLP

Text classification model on federal court opinions. Text mining in SAS Enterprise Miner and an interpretable decision tree to predict legal motion outcomes from linguistic patterns. Published on SSRN.

What I did

  • Built a dataset of 1,535 federal court opinions (D. Conn., 1999 to 2006) with a Python scraper and labeled each motion's outcome.

  • Classified 70.3% of unseen motions correctly; the paper is published on SSRN.
