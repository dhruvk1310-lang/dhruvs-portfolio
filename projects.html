IBM HR Analytics - SQL

Queried and analyzed IBM HR dataset to uncover attrition drivers, compensation patterns, and workforce demographics using advanced SQL.

Legal Motion Outcome Prediction - NLP

Text classification model on federal court opinions. TF-IDF + SVD + scikit-learn to predict legal motion outcomes from linguistic patterns.

What I did

  • Built a dataset of 1,535 federal court opinions (D. Conn., 1999 to 2006) with a Python scraper and labeled each motion's outcome.
  • Turned the text into 22 topic features in SAS Enterprise Miner and trained an interpretable decision tree on them plus case facts.
  • Classified 70.3% of unseen motions correctly; the paper is published on SSRN.

Northeast Heating & Cooling Demand Map

In progress. End-to-end pipeline estimating monthly heating oil, propane and AC demand for all 218 counties across nine Northeast states, from public Census, EIA and NOAA data.

What I did

  • Built a Python pipeline that pulls eight public Census, NOAA and EIA datasets into clean tables, including county-level monthly weather back to 1991.
  • Modeled monthly demand from homes, gallons per heating degree day and prices: an estimated $16.8B market in 2025-26 that peaks every January.
  • Built a SQLite star schema (5 dimensions, 8 facts) and window-function SQL analyses. Found price can beat weather: in 2022-23 spend rose 9% on 4% fewer gallons. Tableau is next.
  • Python
  • SQL
  • SQLite
  • Tableau
  • geopandas
