About Me

Analytics with purpose and precision.

I'm Dhruva Khanna, though most people call me Dhruv. I'm a data analytics professional with an M.S. in Business Analytics from Quinnipiac University and a background in sports retail. What I enjoy most is finding the things we miss: the stories hiding in the data that nobody can see at first glance.

01 · Where it started

A cricket bat at nine

I've played cricket since I was 9, when I watched MS Dhoni hit the winning six in the World Cup final. From then on, cricket was everything. I skipped tuition classes and missed school for practice.

Major ankle injuries meant I never made it to the big stage, but they didn't end my love for sport. So I took a different route into it and studied Sports Management.

02 · Where it clicked

From the shop floor to the dashboard

While finishing my degree, I joined Decathlon in Mumbai as a salesperson on the floor. I learned the business from the ground up and worked my way to managing a department at the biggest store in the city. I was also one of its daily managers, a small group who ran the whole store one or two days a week: staffing, daily operations, safety, cash, security and customers.

Every decision ran through dashboards: which layouts to change, which fast-selling products to stock up on. That's when I understood how much data matters. Using it, I helped cut stockouts by 21.3% and drive a 15.88% revenue increase.

My teammates knew me as the manager who would step in anywhere, selling for another department on a busy day or helping them rework their layout around what was selling across the city and the region.

03 · Quinnipiac

Going all in on data

That pushed me to New Haven for an M.S. in Business Analytics at Quinnipiac University. I graduated in May 2026 with straight As while working on campus as a barista manager at the campus Starbucks.

As Director of Research and Publications for Quinnipiac Blockchain, I published two peer-reviewed papers on SSRN and presented our work at the Yale Global Justice Conference, the moment I'm proudest of from my time there.

04 · What's next

Looking for my next team

I'm looking for a full-time role in data analytics, in any industry where data shapes real decisions. I'm based in New Haven, CT, and open to relocating anywhere in the US. In the meantime, I keep building projects; you'll find them on the Projects page.

A note on work authorization

I'm an international student on an F-1 visa, and my M.S. in Business Analytics is a STEM-designated degree. That means I can work in the US on OPT (Optional Practical Training) for up to three years: 12 months of OPT plus a 24-month STEM extension, in roles related to my field.

Employers don't need to sponsor a visa or file a petition to hire me during that time. For the STEM extension, the employer just needs to be enrolled in E-Verify and complete a training plan with me.
